# Shinext — LinkedIn ↔ Blog distribution map

## Objective
LinkedIn is the distribution channel. The PWA/blog is the canonical destination where the full article, internal links and conversion CTA live.

## Publication rule
1. Create a short LinkedIn hook based on the article.
2. Explain one useful idea without reproducing the complete article.
3. Add a clear invitation to read the full guide.
4. Link to the canonical PWA article URL.
5. End with a relevant Shinext CTA when appropriate.

## Example
**Hook:** “¿Limpieza profunda o mantenimiento? No siempre necesitas lo mismo.”

**Body:** Present one practical distinction and invite the reader to review the complete guide.

**CTA:** “Lee la guía completa en Shinext.”

**Destination:** `/blog/limpieza-profunda-vs-mantenimiento/`

## Operational note
Creating the LinkedIn company page and publishing posts requires an authorized LinkedIn administrator action. This repository stores the editorial map and destination URLs so the social workflow remains repeatable.
